I spent eleven years at Amazon as a tech lead, mostly in distributed systems and conversational AI. The last big thing I built there was the Alexa Plus Gen AI Foundation, which shipped to millions of people. What I took away from it: the model is the easy part. Verification, observability, guardrails — the unglamorous machinery around the AI — is where trust actually comes from.

In 2025 I left to go all-in on agentic development: building tools for AI agents, and agentifying whatever sits still long enough — my dev workflow, my day planning, my own life admin. One idea I keep circling is the **code factory**: a fleet of agents working from a spec, with verification loops tight enough to trust what comes out. I spent the sabbatical building these tools and using them daily. All of it open source.
